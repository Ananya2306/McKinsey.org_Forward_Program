Here are a few reminders before you begin your journey:


Forward Learning Workbook

Download and save the Forward Learning Workbook. Your workbook is an essential companion on your journey during and after the program. Timeline and Roadmap

Forward is a 10-week learning program delivered in a digital and virtual format. Within this digital learning plan, you will find a list of components that you need to complete by a certain deadline. You may start and complete the components at any time during the 10 weeks, as long as you complete them before the deadline stated in your Welcome Email.

You will need between 16 and 20 hours to complete the program. You can distribute the work across the 10 weeks by dedicating 2 hours of learning each week. In your Forward Learning Workbook, you will find a roadmap and planner to help you plan your learning journey.


Live Webinars

In this digital learning plan, you will find 10 different components, with two (2) live webinars. These are:

1. Join a Welcome Ceremony: Welcome Ceremonies are only available during the early days of the program. They are optional and will not affect your progress, but we highly encourage you to join to further understand what the program is all about. Visit Lesson 3 of this module to learn more!

2. Attend the Mandatory Webinar: Lead Forward - Mindsets for Growth: This webinar is mandatory and is one of the requirements for you to graduate. You have several sessions you can choose from, so sign up to the one that fits your schedule best.  All of them are the same, and you only need to fully attend one session.


Badges and Certificates

Badges and certificates will be issued to all participants who have completed the components of the digital learning plan and have attended the mandatory Lead Forward webinar. They will be distributed at the end of the program, only after the full 10 weeks have elapsed, even if you have met the completion requirements in advance.
