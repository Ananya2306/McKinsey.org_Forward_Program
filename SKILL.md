Walking through Layla's journey 

Headshot of Layla
Layla

When Layla was transitioning between jobs, she was concerned about finding a new role. She decided to talk to a coworker who was going through a similar situation.

Play the video below to listen to Layla's conversation with her coworker.



Video Transcript
Coworker: Hey Layla! How are you doing? 



Layla: Hey Nate! Not great to be honest. This round of layoffs is making me nervous. 



Coworker:  I can relate- it is a stressful time. Are you looking at other jobs?



Layla: I am trying but I haven’t had much luck. I need to find a different job where I won’t be as replaceable. All my experience has been in customer service, unfortunately! I have no idea what employers are even looking for.



Coworker:  The last time I was shifting careers, my former manager recommended that I proactively sign up for courses and certifications to learn different skillsets. 



Layla: That is interesting! What kind of skills did you learn?



Coworker: Everything ranging from thinking critically to structuring my communication! These skills are crucial to any career and are typically what most employers are looking for. And while you have to learn other skills, which can certainly be challenging sometimes, keeping your successes in mind can help you through the tough times. 



Layla: This is helpful! I did see an opening for a computer user support specialist that would allow me to work remotely. But I am hesitant to apply.



Coworker:  Why is that?



Layla: To be honest, I feel overwhelmed and slightly resentful towards technology. It seems like it’s always changing and I can never keep up! 



Coworker:  Learning how you can work and think in new ways with data and technology will not only remove the barriers between you and technology, but it will put you in a great place to apply for this job. Of course, you’ll need to continue expanding your digital skills, but a good foundation will get you started to keep learning...

How did Layla get where she is today? 


Changing her mindset to focus on what she can do well and what she would need to learn helped Layla gain the confidence to try something new. She had to become more resilient, bouncing back from obstacles and being comfortable with risks.

Learning digital skills and agile ways of working also helped her feel more in control and in a more comfortable space where she can make technology work for her.
