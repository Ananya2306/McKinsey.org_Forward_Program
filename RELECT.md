Reflect on your Skills

Before you embark on the journey ahead, the Skills Reflection tool will allow you to take a moment to reflect deeply on where you stand with the 10 Future of Work skills covered in the Forward program. By reflecting on the frequency in which you behave in certain ways, you will get insights into your strengths and areas of improvement, which can help you set learning intentions.

A copy of this tool is available in the Skills Reflection section of your Forward Learning Workbook so that you can refer to it whenever needed, especially as you continue to reflect and seek feedback from others.

Steps to complete the Skills Reflection

1
Complete the skills reflection: Read each of the 10 behaviors carefully and indicate how often you have been behaving in that way.

2
Reflect on your responses: Your responses will help you identify your starting point before the program. Identify the strengths to build on and areas for improvement.

3
Seek advice and feedback: Ask a peer, colleague, manager, or client to understand what behaviors they have observed and how that compares to your own self-reflection.

4
Set meaningful intentions: Start making concrete intentions of what you want to practice more often. Use your personal insights, external inputs, and Forward's upcoming learning content.
